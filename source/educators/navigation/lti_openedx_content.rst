Use Open edX Content in Another LMS with LTI
#############################################

Use this path when learners will launch Open edX course content from another
learning management system. In this integration, the Open edX platform acts as
an LTI 1.1 tool provider.

Your site operator must enable the LTI provider functionality and create
credentials before you can add Open edX content to another learning management
system.
