.. _LTI for Educators:

Learning Tools Interoperability (LTI)
#####################################

Use LTI to bring external learning tools into your Open edX course or make
Open edX content available through another platform or LMS that supports LTI.

Choose the path that matches what you want to do:

* :ref:`Add LTI Tools to Course Content` when learners should open a tool from
  a course unit.
* :ref:`Add LTI Tools to Course Navigation` for a course-wide tool such as
  discussions or video conferencing.
* :ref:`Use Open edX Content in Another LMS with LTI` when learners will access
  Open edX content from another platform or LMS.
